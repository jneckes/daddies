02 / RUN OF SHOW

BUILT IN L.A.
ON A DREAM.

Somewhere between school drop-off and a 9 a.m. meeting, four dads looked at each other and said: we’ve still got it. Nobody asked us to prove it. We booked the gig anyway.

The Daddies are a Los Angeles institution (self-declared) with one mission: recapture the songs of your youth, recast the songs of today, and leave every dance floor slightly better parented than we found it. The lineup rotates. The commitment doesn’t. Somebody always brings extra cables.

“Dads are out here playing music. It’s sick.”

— JOSH, DADDY No. 04

SAMPLE setlists

Three ways a night with The Daddies can go. Requests honored. Slow songs deployed strategically.

THE DADDIES — SET A — “THE 90s SET” — flannel optional, angst included

  1. MY NAME IS JONAS — Weezer the sweater song is a different song
  2. HARD TO HANDLE — The Black Crowes
  3. DAMMIT — blink-182 well, we guess this is growing up
  4. ARE YOU GONNA GO MY WAY — Lenny Kravitz leather pants sold separately
  5. MR. BRIGHTSIDE — The Killers technically 2004. spiritually 90s.
  6. 1979 — The Smashing Pumpkins some daddies remember it firsthand
  7. SEMI-CHARMED LIFE — Third Eye Blind doo doo doo. you know the rest.
  8. WHAT’S UP? — 4 Non Blondes group scream therapy
  9. WHAT I GOT — Sublime

  11. UNINVITED — Alanis Morissette the milwaukee headliner

  9. WITH OR WITHOUT YOU — U2 slow song, deployed strategically
